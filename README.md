# Thimble

A calm, private to-do app for Mac and Android.

Thimble keeps your tasks on your own devices. There's no account to make, no server to trust, and nothing to sign in to. When you sync, your tasks travel as encrypted files through a folder you choose, sealed with a passphrase only you know.

## What it does

- **Capture in seconds.** On the Mac, press Control-Space from any app, or use the menu bar, the Share menu, or Shortcuts. On Android, use the Quick Settings tile, the Today widget, or share from any app.
- **Plan your day.** Today, This Evening, Upcoming, Anytime, and Someday, with deadlines, repeating tasks, and reminders. Type dates the way you say them: "tomorrow", "next friday", "in 2 weeks".
- **Keep projects moving.** Areas, projects, headings, checklists, tags, and notes in Markdown. Finished work goes to the Logbook.
- **See where your projects stand.** Give a project a card with its platforms and versions, and the Dashboard compares them all: what's open, what needs you, what's blocked, and what closed this week.
- **See your calendar** next to your tasks in Today and Upcoming. Events are read, never saved.
- **Use it your way.** Seven colors, light and dark, English and French, and a full set of keyboard shortcuts on the Mac.

## Private by design

- No account, no analytics, no crash reports, no ads.
- The Mac app's only connection is checking for its own updates. The Android app asks for no internet access at all.
- Sync uses AES-256 encryption with your passphrase. The service that carries the folder sees only sealed files.
- Lock Thimble with Touch ID or your phone's screen lock, and hide reminder text on the lock screen.
- Export everything to plain files whenever you like, and keep daily backups in a folder you choose.

## Questions and problems

See the [help page](HELP.md) first. If something's wrong, [open an issue](https://github.com/matguitarist/thimble/issues) and say which device and version you're on. Please don't include your tasks.

This repository holds the releases and the update feed. The source isn't published.
